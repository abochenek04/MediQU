MediQU's adapted dataset contains only US populated places and administrative
seats with at least 50,000 residents in this source snapshot. Neighborhoods,
historical places and other feature types are omitted. Population figures are
source estimates and may differ from current census counts.

Each cities.json row is [latitude, longitude, name, US state, population].
Coordinates are rounded public city centers, not user locations.

Within 5 km of a listed town/city center, use its name. Otherwise prefer the
nearest major city (population at least 250,000) within 100 km. If none is within
100 km, use the nearest listed town within that distance. This gives larger towns
their own label while smaller suburbs use a recognizable nearby city.

This is an approximate regional label, not a municipal boundary lookup.
No city is returned if all listed centers are more than 100 km away.
This data is provided as is, without guarantees of accuracy or completeness.

The directory is served as a static asset. User coordinates are matched in the
browser and are never sent to GeoNames or MediQU for the city lookup.
